# Hunter System

A Solo Leveling style habit tracker. Complete daily quests, earn EXP and gold, level up your hunter, raise a shadow army and clear dungeon floors.

**Works offline.** After the first visit, everything runs on your phone with no internet needed.

## Install on your phone

1. Open the app link in your browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone).
2. **Android:** tap the menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
   **iPhone:** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the new Hunter icon. It runs full screen, like a normal app.

## Your progress

- Everything is saved on your own device. Nobody else can see it, and each friend has their own hunter.
- Back it up from **Settings › Backup › Export backup**. Use **Import backup** to move to a new phone.
- Clearing your browser's site data deletes your progress, so keep a backup.

## What's inside

- Daily quests with ranks E to S, mini versions, weekly target increases and day-by-day plans (gym splits, stretching, martial arts drills)
- Prayers panel, battle tracker, gates (big one-time goals), real-world tests and titles
- Armory with ranked gear, sets and stats, 13 themes
- Dungeon tab: fights, bosses with drops, a shadow army you can evolve, and skills
